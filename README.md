## Analytical Methodology & Frameworks

Every document in this repository follows a structured, evidence-based methodology designed to deliver consulting-grade insights:

```
  +-------------------+      +-------------------+      +-------------------+      +-------------------+
  |   Data & Context  | ---> | Root Cause & Math | ---> | Strategic Options | ---> | 90-Day Execution  |
  |    Gathering      |      |     Modeling      |      |   & Prioritization|      |    ROI Tracking   |
  +-------------------+      +-------------------+      +-------------------+      +-------------------+
```

- **First-Principles Financial Modeling:** Evaluating sales initiatives based on Net Present Value (NPV), Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) Payback, Pipeline Velocity, and Lifetime Value (LTV) multiples rather than top-line impressions.
- **Root-Cause Diagnostic Mapping:** Identifying the underlying operational, structural, or behavioral bottlenecks behind plateaued top-line growth.
- **Segmented TAM/SAM/SOM Sizing:** Utilizing bottom-up and top-down market sizing techniques grounded in New Zealand tech sector data (e.g., TIN200 reports, Stats NZ enterprise numbers).
- **Actionable Execution Roadmaps:** Translating high-level strategy into concrete 30-60-90 day implementation schedules with measurable key performance indicators (KPIs).

## Case Study Directory

### 1. [The Quantitative Cost of Slow Lead Response Time](./01-cost-of-slow-lead-response.md)
* **Focus:** Revenue Operations & Lead Conversion Optimization
* **Core Question:** What is the exact financial cost of delayed lead response times for a medium-sized B2B sales organization?
* **Key Findings:** Responding to inbound inquiries within 5 minutes yields a 21x higher qualification rate compared to a 30-minute delay. A representative $5M ARR company leaks over $420,000 annually in missed contract value due to average response times exceeding 4 hours.
* **Key Deliverables:** Mathematical leakage formula, multi-scenario revenue impact table, 5-step operational overhaul, 60-day implementation Gantt chart.

### 2. [Commercial Sales Diagnostic: KiwiTech Solutions](./02-nz-retail-sales-diagnostic.md)
* **Focus:** B2B Commercial Diagnostic & Turnaround Strategy
* **Core Question:** How can a mid-market New Zealand SaaS provider ("KiwiTech Solutions") overcome an $8.5M ARR growth plateau and high customer churn?
* **Key Findings:** Growth stagnated due to misaligned SMB/Enterprise outbound targeting, a 35% revenue leakage during onboarding handoffs, and rampant unapproved discounting reducing Gross Margins by 12%.
* **Key Deliverables:** Comprehensive current-state financial assessment, 3-tier root cause analysis, re-segmented GTM strategy, projected ARR expansion model ($8.5M to $12.1M over 18 months), and a 90-day execution roadmap.

### 3. [NZ B2B SaaS Market Opportunity Analysis](./03-market-opportunity-saas-nz.md)
* **Focus:** Market Intelligence & Go-To-Market (GTM) Strategy
* **Core Question:** Where are the highest-margin growth opportunities in the NZ B2B software sector, and how should mid-market vendors position themselves?
* **Key Findings:** Total Addressable Market (TAM) for NZ B2B SaaS reaches $2.4B, with the mid-market segment (50–500 employees) offering the optimal risk-adjusted expansion pathway due to lower CAC and fast sales cycles.
* **Key Deliverables:** TAM/SAM/SOM breakdowns, competitive positioning matrix, trans-Tasman scaling framework, risk matrix with mitigation levers.

### 4. [Sales Metrics That Actually Matter: Beyond Vanity Analytics](./04-sales-metrics-that-matter.md)
* **Focus:** Sales Performance Analytics & Governance
* **Core Question:** Which sales metrics provide true predictive power versus misleading vanity indicators?
* **Key Findings:** Activity volume (dials/emails) lacks correlation with quota attainment without pipeline velocity weighting. Tracking Speed-to-First-Touch, Stage-Weighted Pipeline Velocity, and CAC Payback Period yields 3.2x greater forecasting accuracy.
* **Key Deliverables:** Deconstruction of common vanity metrics, deep dives into 7 core operational metrics (formulas, benchmarks, red flags), dashboard layout specification, and data governance checklist.
